# 🎵 aTunes Legacy

> *The Ultimate Retro Media Experience — Classic iOS & iTunes 11 Design Reborn for Android.*

---

## ✨ Welcome to aTunes Legacy

Step back into the golden age of digital media management! **aTunes Legacy** combines the timeless, gorgeous skeuomorphic aesthetic of classic iPodOS / iOS 6 and iTunes 11 with powerful modern playback capabilities. Whether you're curating local music, watching movies and TV shows, browsing camera photos, recording voice memos, or rediscovering album art in 3D Cover Flow, aTunes Legacy delivers the ultimate nostalgia trip packed with robust features.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Functions

### 1. 🎨 Multi-Theme Engine & Classic iOS Settings
* **4 Distinct Themes**: Switch between **Legacy Light** (iTunes 11 silver metallic), **Legacy Dark** (sleek dark retro), **New Light** (modern Apple Music aesthetic), and **New Dark** (dark mode Apple Music).
* **Classic iOS Settings Redesign**: Modeled after vintage iOS 6 settings with grouped rounded list cells, icons, blue chevrons, and smooth toggle switches.
* **Theme-Aware Input Styling**: Text boxes automatically adapt their text color (black on light themes, white on dark themes) for perfect contrast.

### 2. 🎛️ 3D Cover Flow (Landscape Mode)
* **Immersive Rotation**: Rotate your device into landscape orientation to instantly launch **Cover Flow**.
* **Momentum & Physics Scrolling**: Swipe right and left across the screen to spin through your album, artist, or playlist covers in stunning 3D perspective.
* **Interactive Flipping**: Tap the center album cover to flip it around and reveal the complete tracklist on the back. Tap any song to start playback immediately and queue subsequent tracks.

### 3. 🎶 Advanced Music Player & Library
* **Embedded & Sideloaded Artwork**: Prioritizes high-resolution embedded artwork tags with automatic fallback to `cover.jpg` or `folder.png` files in the media directory.
* **Full Transport Controls**: Integrated play/pause, next track, previous track, and progress scrubber available in the top bar LCD, Music Player overlay, and lock screen.
* **Gestures & Controls**: Swipe over album art to skip tracks, slide vertically for volume control, and double-tap to toggle lyrics.
* **Smart Playlists & Backup**: Automatically generated smart playlists ("Most Played", "Recently Added", decade brackets) plus easy export/import backup of playlists, play counts, ratings, and equalizer presets.

### 4. 🎬 Tactile Video Player
* **Swipe Gestures**: Slide up/down on the left side for screen brightness, right side for volume control, and swipe horizontally anywhere to scrub with precise frame previews.
* **Aspect Ratio Scaling**: Instantly switch between original size, stretch-to-fill, fit-to-screen, and cinematic 16:9/21:9 crops.
* **PiP & Background Playback**: Minimize into a resizable Picture-in-Picture window or turn off your screen while playing music videos to continue background audio.
* **A-B Repeat & Frame Scrubbing**: Pause and advance single frames or loop specific segments infinitely.

### 5. 📸 Photos Tab
* Automatically scans and imports photos stored in your device's `DCIM > Camera` folder, displaying them in a gorgeous grid with legacy aTunes styling.

### 6. 🎙️ Recordings Tab & Voice Recorder
* Built-in voice recorder with **Record**, **Pause**, and **Stop** functions.
* Saves recordings directly to `Internal Storage > Recordings > aTunes`.
* **Metadata Editor**: Long-press any recording in the list to open a popup where you can edit its name, date, and tag, or delete the file.
* Play previous voice memos directly using the built-in music player.

### 7. 🎮 Games & Apps Tab
* Display installed app shortcuts in a sleek grid.
* Launch any app instantly with a tap.
* Long-press any app shortcut to remove it from your aTunes Games tab.
* Import apps/shortcuts via **Settings > Add Media to Library > Games (Apps)**.

### 8. 👤 User Profile & Statistics
* Dedicated Profile page accessible from Settings.
* **User Identity**: Custom avatar/PFP upload, display name, and personal status/bio ("Vinyl Collector").
* **Offline Storage Usage**: Visual storage breakdown bar tracking local music space usage.
* **Listening Insights**: Local statistics showing top tracks, top albums, top artists, total listening hours, and fun milestones/badges.
* **Library Summary**: Quick track, album, artist, and playlist count overview plus recently added tracks.

## 📱 How to Use aTunes Legacy

1. **Navigation**: Use the horizontally scrollable bottom navigation bar to switch between **Music**, **TV Shows**, **Movies**, **Podcasts**, **Audiobooks**, **Photos**, **Recordings**, **Games**, and **Settings**.
2. **Adding Media**: Go to **Settings > Add Media to Library** to import local music, videos, audiobooks, podcasts, or app shortcuts.
3. **Playback**: Tap any media item to play it in the built-in audio or video player. Use the top bar retro LCD display to control playback or open full-screen players.
4. **Cover Flow**: Turn your phone sideways (landscape mode) at any time in Music to experience Cover Flow.
5. **Customization**: Visit **Settings > Theme & Appearance** to choose your preferred theme. Tap your profile card in Settings to view your listening stats and change your avatar.
